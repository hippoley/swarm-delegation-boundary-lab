# Research question and hypotheses (pre-sprint)

RQ: Across multi-hop delegation chains, how often can downstream agents perform actions outside the root's permitted scope?

H1: Self-reported permissions can allow over-broad action acceptance under adversarial synthetic scenarios.

H2: Explicit intersection of effective upstream authority and delegated permissions should prevent simple widening, assuming a trusted enforcement point.

H3: Identity binding, revocation and replay checks may close additional gaps, at measurable complexity and latency cost.

All hypotheses are falsifiable. No result has yet been measured.
